Tokens fail when they are suggestions. MyBizAI treats them as **constraints**:

- Cobalt / Ultramarine `#120a8f` — trust surfaces
- Dark orange `#ff8c00` — primary CTAs only
- Gold `#d4af37` — rare emphasis and borders

Export a kit from the [Brand Identity Kit](/brand-kit) when the system feels locked.
